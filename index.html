REISSAD STUDIO’S BODYCAM (v0.8 LOCKED & LOADED) — TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Developer: Reissad Studio (founded by French indie developers Luca & Léo). Built on Unreal Engine 5, released in Early Access June 7, 2024, with the landmark v0.8 "Locked & Loaded" overhaul launched September 2, 2026.

  • True Body & Unlinked Free-Aim Deadzone: Unlike standard FPS games where the gun is glued to the screen center, Bodycam mounts a wide-angle fisheye camera to the operator’s chest while arms and weapons pivot independently inside a physical deadzone with spring-damper inertia. Bullets travel along the true 3D barrel vector.
  • v0.8 Stress-Driven Dynamic Reloads: A real-time Stress parameter calculated from suppression, nearby explosions, and combat intensity alters reload animations—causing operators under pressure (>50% stress) to fumble magazines, shake, and take longer to chamber rounds.
  • No HUD & Diegetic Mag Checks: No traditional crosshairs or digital ammo counters. Operators rely on physical iron sights/optics, visible laser beams, flashlights, and pulling the magazine out slightly ([M]) to check remaining brass rounds.
  • v0.8 Arsenal & 8-Effect Attachment System: 24+ weapons across Assault Rifles (SKR-H, M4, KA-74, KA-74M, M16-1A, Drako), SMGs (Veaper 1200 RPM, SG9-X, SG5-K, UZ-1), Shotguns (BK-101, BK-102S, Vaiga, R12), Snipers (Rivington 700 bolt-action, VSD, LAR), and Pistols (Mlock19, Deagle, CR-75, M1914, Revolver). Attachments modify ADS Speed, Switch Speed, Reload Speed, Horizontal/Vertical Recoil, Spread, Kick, and Capacity.
  • Tactical Tablet, FPV Drones & RC Cars: Menus live inside an in-world Tactical Tablet. Operators deploy FPV Drones (with Stabilized Casual or Full-Manual Acro physics) and explosive RC Cars, while their operator body remains vulnerable in the world.
  • Law-Enforcement Redacted Faces & Ragdoll Stumbling: Operator faces feature pixelated mosaic blur, and non-lethal hits trigger physical Ragdoll Stumbling before collapse.
