REISSAD STUDIO’S BODYCAM (v0.8 LOCKED & LOADED) — TECHNICAL ANALYSIS

Developer: Reissad Studio (founded by French indie developers Luca & Léo). Built on Unreal Engine 5, released in Early Access June 7, 2024, with the landmark v0.8 "Locked & Loaded" overhaul launched September 2, 2026.

  • True Body & Unlinked Free-Aim Deadzone: Unlike standard FPS games where the gun is glued to the screen center, Bodycam mounts a wide-angle fisheye camera to the operator’s chest while arms and weapons pivot independently inside a physical deadzone with spring-damper inertia. Bullets travel along the true 3D barrel vector.
  • v0.8 Stress-Driven Dynamic Reloads: A real-time Stress parameter calculated from suppression, nearby explosions, and combat intensity alters reload animations—causing operators under pressure (>48% stress) to fumble magazines, shake, and take longer to chamber rounds.
  • Canted Point-Shooting & Obstacle Barrel Compression: Operators can tilt rifles into a 38° Canted Laser stance ([X] or CANT button), and stepping close to walls physically compresses the weapon into High-Port rather than clipping.
  • v0.8 Arsenal & 8-Effect Attachment System: 18+ weapons across Assault Rifles, SMGs, Shotguns, Snipers, and Pistols with attachments affecting ADS Speed, Switch Speed, Reload Speed, Horizontal/Vertical Recoil, Spread, Kick, and Capacity.
